# Employee Handbook (sample)

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Staff may work remotely up to three days per week.

## Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next year.

## Expenses
Expenses above 100 USD need written approval from a manager before purchase. Receipts must be submitted within 14 days.
